BooxDrop Device for Calibre
===========================

Send books from Calibre to a BOOX e-reader over your local network using
the BooxDrop wireless transfer feature built into BOOX devices.

The plugin appears as a wireless device in Calibre's device toolbar
whenever your BOOX is reachable on the same network. Use the Send to
Device action to push books; uploads stream in 64 KB chunks with
per-byte progress reporting and a pre-flight free-space check.

Configuration: Preferences > Plugins > BooxDrop Device. Set the URL
shown on the BOOX device's BooxDrop screen (form: http://host:port).

Source: https://github.com/fmcurti/calibre-booxdrop
